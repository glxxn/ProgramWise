TCO presentation

The presentation compares AWS EC2 (IaaS) to Wix (SaaS). Over 5 years, running on EC2 costs $1,177 whereas Wix's light plan costs $1,092. AWS EC2 was selected for ProgramWise because it offered greater control and scalability than Wix, which was limited in flexibility and customisation.
